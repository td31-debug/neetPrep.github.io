# NEET PULSE 3D - Interactive 12th Grade Medical & Sciences Platform

An interactive visual learning platform built for 12th-grade NEET aspirants, featuring 3D moveable anatomical and molecular models, interactive labelled diagrams with Active Recall quiz mode, visual real-time concept simulators, a comprehensive 84-question NEET PYQ Exam Engine, and an in-depth Aakash Chemistry Study Module for Chapter 8 (Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids).

🌐 **Live GitHub Pages URL**: [https://td31-debug.github.io/neetPrep.github.io/](https://td31-debug.github.io/neetPrep.github.io/)

---

## 🚀 Features & Modules

### 1. 📖 Aakash Chemistry Study Hub (Chapter 8: Aldehydes, Ketones and Carboxylic Acids)
- **Scrollytelling Split-Screen Layout**: The interactive visual stage on the left updates dynamically in real time as you scroll through the 9 topics on the right.
- **Deep Curriculum Coverage**:
  1. Introduction & Natural Occurrence (Vanillin, Cinnamaldehyde, Salicylaldehyde)
  2. Nomenclature & Carbonyl Group Orbital Architecture ($sp^2$ trigonal planar, dipole moment $\mu \approx 2.3-2.8\text{ D}$)
  3. Preparation Matrix of Aldehydes & Ketones (Rosenmund, Stephen, DIBAL-H, Etard, Gattermann-Koch, Ozonolysis)
  4. Physical Properties (Boiling point hierarchy & hydrogen bonding solubility)
  5. Chemical Properties, Uses & Distinction Tests (Interactive Tollens' Silver Mirror, Fehling's red $\text{Cu}_2\text{O}$ ppt, Iodoform test, Aldol vs Cannizzaro)
  6. Carboxylic Acids Structure & Carboxyl Resonance
  7. Methods for Preparation of Carboxylic Acids (Alkylbenzene oxidation, Grignard with dry ice, nitrile hydrolysis)
  8. Physical Properties (8-membered cyclic dimer structure & elevated boiling points)
  9. Chemical Properties & Acidity Ladder ($pK_a$ ranking, HVZ $\alpha$-halogenation, soda-lime decarboxylation)
- **Embedded Checkpoint Quizzes**: Instant answer check and audio feedback.

### 2. 🪐 3D Moveable Models Lab
- **Water Molecule (H₂O)**: V-shaped bent geometry (104.5°) with hydrogen-bonding dipole network.
- **Methane Molecule (CH₄)**: Tetrahedral 109.5° geometry with zero net dipole moment.
- **DNA Double Helix (B-DNA)**: Watson-Crick antiparallel double helix with complementary base pairs.
- **3D Beating Human Heart**: Double circulation and chambers with real-time BPM pulse slider (40–150 BPM).
- **Bohr Hydrogen Atom**: Nucleus with quantized stationary orbits ($r_n \propto n^2$) and interactive Quantum Jump buttons ($n=1, 2, 3, 4$).

### 3. 📐 Labelled Diagrams & Active Recall
- **Master Mole Concept Roadmap** (Mass, Moles, STP Volume, Avogadro Particles).
- **Human Heart Internal Anatomy** (Pacemaker SA node, valves, chambers).
- **Nephron & Counter-Current Mechanism** (Cortex, medulla, Henle's loop gradient).
- **Ray Optics & Lens Refraction** (Convex/concave lenses, $2F_1$ real inverted image, thin lens formula).
- **Active Recall Quiz Mode**: Tests landmark recognition with instant audio/visual scoring.

### 4. 🧪 Visual Concept Simulators
- **Stoichiometry & Limiting Reagent Sandbox**: Real-time particle collisions and yield calculations for 5 NEET reactions.
- **Solution Concentration & Beaker Simulator**: Interactive beaker with live Molarity, Molality, Normality, and Mole Fraction calculations.
- **Projectile Motion & Parabolic Trajectory Sandbox**: Live launch angle and speed sliders calculating Time of Flight $T$, Max Height $H$, and Range $R$.
- **Significant Figures & Scientific Notation Master**: Real-time digit breakdown.

### 5. 📝 NEET PYQ Examination Engine (84 High-Yield Questions)
- 64 Chemistry Questions (38 Years PYQs from Chapter 1 with full step-by-step NCERT solutions).
- 10 High-Yield Biology Questions (Genetics, Circulation, Excretion, Photosynthesis).
- 10 High-Yield Physics Questions (Optics, Bohr Model, Projectiles, Measurements, Capacitance).
- Instant Practice Mode vs 45-Minute Timed Mock Exam (+4 / -1 marking scheme).
